## 1. PSARA ACT 2005 — FULL DETAILS

### The Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (No. 29 of 2005)

**Source:** https://uppolice.gov.in/Site-Content/uploaded-content/pdf/private_security_agencies_etc_act_2005.pdf
**Source:** https://haryanapolice.gov.in/pdf/Private_Security_Agencies_Act_2005.pdf
**Source:** https://lawgist.in/private-security-agencies-regulation-act

**Key Facts:**
- Passed by Rajya Sabha: 10th May 2005
- Passed by Lok Sabha: 12th May 2005
- Received President's assent: June 23, 2005
- Came into force: March 14, 2006 (date appointed by Central Government)
- Extends to whole of India EXCEPT the State of Jammu and Kashmir (Note: J&K now covered post-2019 reorganization)
- 25 Sections total

### Section-by-Section Summary:

**Section 1 — Short title, extent, commencement:** Called the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005.

**Section 2 — Definitions:**
- (a) "armoured car service" — service by deployment of armed guards with armoured car
- (b) "Controlling Authority" — officer designated under Section 3
- (c) "licence" — granted under Section 7(5)
- (f) "private security" — security by non-public servant to protect person/property
- (g) "private security agency" — person/body other than government agency engaged in providing private security services including training
- (h) "private security guard" — person providing private security with/without arms, includes supervisor

**Section 3 — Appointment of Controlling Authority:**
- State Government shall designate an officer NOT below the rank of Joint Secretary in Home Department
- State Government may provide additional officers/staff

**Section 4 — License Mandatory:**
- "No person shall carry on or commence the business of private security agency, unless he holds a licence issued under this Act"
- Existing agencies at commencement had 1 year grace period if application filed within that period
- No private security agency shall provide private security abroad without permission of Controlling Authority (which shall consult Central Government)

**Section 5 — Eligibility for licence:**
- Application considered only after due verification of antecedents

**Section 6 — Persons NOT eligible for licence:**
- (1)(a) Convicted of offence in connection with promotion/formation/management of a company (fraud/misfeasance), including undischarged insolvent
- (1)(b) Convicted by competent court for offence with punishment of imprisonment NOT less than two years
- (1)(c) Keeping links with banned organization/association posing threat to national security/public order
- (1)(d) Dismissed/removed from government service on grounds of misconduct/moral turpitude
- (2) Company/firm/association NOT eligible if: not registered in India, OR having proprietor/majority shareholder/partner/director who is NOT a citizen of India

**Section 7 — Application for grant of licence:**
- (1) Application to Controlling Authority in prescribed form
- (2) Applicant must submit affidavit covering Section 6 details, training availability, Section 11 conditions, police cases/pending court cases
- (3) Fee structure:
  - (a) ₹5,000 — operating in ONE district
  - (b) ₹10,000 — operating in more than one but up to FIVE districts
  - (c) ₹25,000 — operating in WHOLE STATE
- (4) Controlling Authority shall grant/refuse within 60 days from receipt of complete application with fee
- (5) Licence valid for 5 years unless cancelled, renewable for further 5 years

**Section 8 — Renewal of licence:**
- Application not less than 45 days before expiry
- Controlling Authority shall pass order within 30 days from receipt of complete application

**Section 9 — Conditions for commencement & supervisors:**
- Agency must commence activities within 6 months of obtaining licence
- Must ensure prescribed training for guards and supervisors
- Must employ prescribed number of supervisors within 60 days of licence
- Preference to ex-servicemen (Army, Navy, Air Force, Armed Forces, State Police, Home Guards — 3+ years experience) as supervisors

**Section 10 — Eligibility to be a private security guard:**
- Must be citizen of India
- Age: 18 to 65 years
- Must have completed prescribed security training successfully
- No person convicted or dismissed on grounds of misconduct/moral turpitude from armed forces/state police/government/private security agency shall be employed
- Preference to ex-servicemen

**Section 11 — Conditions of licence:**
- State Government may frame rules for training, licensee details, address change, management change, criminal charges

**Section 12 — Licence to be exhibited:**
- Every agency shall exhibit licence/copy in conspicuous place of business

**Section 13 — Cancellation and suspension of licence:**
- Controlling Authority may cancel on various grounds
- Suspension up to 30 days pending cancellation proceedings
- Show cause notice mandatory

**Section 14 — Appeals:**
- Appeal to Home Secretary of State Government within 60 days of order

**Section 15 — Register to be maintained:**
- Names/addresses of persons managing agency
- Names/addresses/photographs/salaries of guards and supervisors
- Names/addresses of clients
- Other prescribed particulars

**Section 16 — Inspection:**
- Controlling Authority can enter premises, inspect records/accounts/documents

**Section 17 — Photo identity card:**
- Every guard must be issued photo identity card

**Section 18 — Disclosure of information:**
- Guard shall not divulge information acquired during employment except as required under Act or police inquiry

**Section 20 — Punishment:**
- Contravention of Section 4: imprisonment up to 1 year, or fine up to ₹25,000, or both

**Section 22 — Offences by companies:**
- Every person in charge at time of offence deemed guilty
- Director/manager/secretary/officer also liable if offence committed with consent/connivance

**Section 24 — Central Government may frame model rules**

**Section 25 — Power of State Government to make rules**

## 2. PSARA LICENSE — PROCESS, ELIGIBILITY, DOCUMENTS, FEES, TIMELINE

### What is PSARA License?
**Source:** https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/psara-license-india-complete-guide-security-agencies.html
**Source:** https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/psara-license-costtimeline-state-rules-india.html

- PSARA License = legal permission granted by State Controlling Authority to operate a private security agency
- Mandatory for any person/firm/LLP/company providing security guards, supervisors, bodyguards, bouncers, armed guards
- Governed by Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005
- Issued by Controlling Authority of respective State/UT

### Who Needs It?
- Any person/business providing private security services
- Security guard agencies, bouncer service providers, bodyguard agencies
- Event security agencies, corporate security providers
- Manpower companies supplying guards
- Agencies serving offices, factories, malls, hotels, hospitals, housing societies

### Complete Step-by-Step Process (11 Steps):

**Source:** https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/psara-license-india-step-by-step-process.html

**Step 1: Decide Business Structure**
- Options: Proprietorship, Partnership Firm, LLP, Private Limited Company
- Choice depends on business plan, number of promoters, investment, liability preference, future expansion
- For professional operations: LLP or Private Limited Company preferred

**Step 2: Include Security Services in Business Objects**
- MOA must mention security services/manpower supply/guard services
- LLP agreement/partnership deed must include private security services
- If object clause doesn't include security services → amendment required

**Step 3: Arrange Office Premises**
- Owned, rented, or leased premises
- Documents: ownership proof, rent agreement, electricity bill, property tax receipt, NOC from owner, office photographs
- Address must match across ALL documents (business docs, GST, rent agreement, PSARA application)

**Step 4: Prepare Basic Business Documents**
- PAN card, GST registration, Certificate of Incorporation/partnership deed/LLP agreement
- MOA, AOA, registered office proof, bank account details, board resolution
- Identity/address proof of promoters/directors/partners/proprietor
- Business profile, proposed area of operation, manpower plan

**Step 5: Police Verification of Applicant & Key Persons**
- Background check of applicant/directors/partners/proprietor/principal officer
- Ensure identity proof, address proof, photographs, affidavits are accurate

**Step 6: Enter into Training Arrangement (MOU)**
- Sign MOU with recognized/approved training institute
- Guards must be trained before deployment

**Step 7: Appoint Supervisors**
- Must have proper supervision over guards at client locations
- Supervisors monitor performance, attendance, discipline, duty rotation

**Step 8: Prepare Affidavits and Declarations**
- Confirm eligibility and compliance with PSARA
- Confirm no disqualification, no serious criminal background
- Format as prescribed by state authority

**Step 9: File PSARA License Application**
- File with State/UT Controlling Authority
- Online or physical depending on state procedure
- Include: agency details, registered office, area of operation, promoters/directors/partners, training arrangement, supervisors, staff structure
- Pay government fee according to area of operation

**Step 10: Scrutiny by Controlling Authority**
- Reviews application, documents, police verification, training arrangement
- May raise queries → respond quickly

**Step 11: Grant of PSARA License**
- If satisfied → license granted
- Agency can operate within approved area subject to license conditions

### Eligibility Criteria:

**For Agency:**
- Must be registered in India
- Business name must include "Security Services" or "Security Agency" or related terms
- For companies: MOA must include security services as main object
- Must have proper office premises
- Must have MOU with recognized training institute
- Must have financial capacity

**For Directors/Principal Officers:**
- Indian citizen
- Minimum 18 years of age (some sources say 21)
- Clean criminal record
- Not convicted of serious offences (punishment of 2+ years imprisonment)
- Not dismissed from government service on grounds of misconduct/moral turpitude
- No links with banned organizations
- Must pass police verification
- 8th standard pass or above (per SetIndiaBiz)
- Must have special security training certificate

**For Security Guards:**
- Indian citizen
- Age: 18 to 65 years
- Must complete prescribed security training
- Must pass police verification
- No criminal conviction
- Not dismissed from government service
- Must meet physical fitness standards

| Area of Operation | Government Fee |
|-------------------|---------------|
| One District | ₹5,000 |
| More than 1 up to 5 Districts | ₹10,000 |
| Entire State / More than 5 Districts | ₹25,000 |

**NOTE:** Karnataka state fee for entire state is ₹50,000 (as per multiple sources).

### Total Timeline:
- **Overall:** 45–90 days typically (up to 120 days in some cases)
- **Document preparation:** 7–15 days
- **Training MOU:** 3–10 days
- **Application filing:** 1–3 days
- **Police verification:** 15–45 days (most time-consuming)
- **Authority scrutiny:** 15–30 days
- **License issuance:** Within 7 days after approval in some states (Haryana)

### Validity:
- **Most states:** 5 years from date of issuance
- **Exceptions (1 year validity):** Uttarakhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh
- Renewal must be applied at least 45 days before expiry (some states say 90 days)

### STATE-SPECIFIC DETAILS:

#### RAJASTHAN
**Source:** https://home.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/HomeGuardsfile/PrivateSecurity/private%20security%20agencies%20rules%202022%20gazette.pdf
- Rules: Rajasthan Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Rules, 2022
- Training: 100 hours classroom + 60 hours field training for entry level (20 working days)
- Ex-servicemen: 40 hours classroom + 16 hours field training (7 working days)
- Training syllabus includes: VIP Security, Internal Security, Institutional Security, Fire Fighting, Disaster Management, Access Control, Anti-Sabotage Checks, Legal provisions (PSARA Act, Labour Laws)
- Renewal conditions: must maintain principal place of business in Rajasthan; must continue training availability
- Forms: Form-I (Application), Form-II (Antecedent verification), Form-IV (License)

#### HARYANA
**Source:** https://haryanapolice.gov.in/pdf/How_to_APPLY_for_PSARA_licence.pdf
- Controlling Authority: Additional Director General of Police (Law & Order), Haryana Police Headquarters, Sector-6, Panchkula-134109
- Email: adgp.laworder@hry.nic.in | Tel: 0172-2585724
- Application in Form-V with fee via DD/Banker's Cheque in name of "Director General of Police, Haryana"
- Also required: Form-I for antecedent verification (separate for each proprietor/director/partner)
- Principal Place of Business must be at commercial location (NOT residential)
- Documents list includes 24 items
- Special qualification: Certificate from SKSDC/DGR/DGET/recognized university/institute OR discharge certificate of Army/Air Force/Navy/Paramilitary/Police OR proof of having operated PSA earlier for 1+ year
- Also requires MOU-2 for annual medical examination with reputed hospital
- Uniform must be distinctly different from defence/paramilitary/police
- PSARA licence issued within a week on receipt of complete documents

#### MAHARASHTRA
**Source:** https://uncomplycate.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Maharashtra-Private-Security-Agencies-Regulations-Rules-2022.pdf
- Rules: Maharashtra Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Rules, 2022 (superseded 2007 rules)
- Application: Form I + Form II (antecedent verification) + Form III (Affidavit)
- Controlling Authority sends copy to District SP or DCP zone for verification of premises
- Must use CCTNS and ICJS databases for antecedent verification
- Prior state license holders: antecedent reverification not needed if license still valid
- Physical copy of license delivered by post within 15 days
- Training: 100 hours classroom + 60 hours field training (20 working days)
- Ex-servicemen/former police: 40 hours classroom + 16 hours field training (7 working days)
- Character verification via Form V and Form VI (Affidavit)
- Character/antecedent verification report issued within 15 days

#### TAMIL NADU
**Source:** http://www.eagle4ss.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/tamilnadu_2005.pdf
- Rules: Tamil Nadu Private Security Agencies Rules, 2008
- Competent Authority: Inspector General of Police, Tamil Nadu Commando Force
- Application: Form V to Controlling Authority
- Fee via DD/Banker's Cheque payable to "Controlling Authority, Tamil Nadu Private Security Agency, Chennai"
- Physical standards: minimum chest 81cm (unexpanded)/86cm (expanded), weight 50kg, height 168cm; must hear spoken voice and alarms
- Medical examination every 12 months
- Supervisors: 1 supervisor per 15 guards; if different premises, at least 1 per 6 guards
- Licensee must undergo training as prescribed by Controlling Authority
- Renewal: same form as new application; same fee as grant
- Appeal fee: ₹500

#### KARNATAKA
**Source:** http://kssa.in/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Karnataka-Private-Security-Agencies-Rules2008.pdf
- Rules: Karnataka Private Security Agencies Rules, 2008
- Application: Form V with Form I for antecedent verification (separate for each proprietor/majority shareholder/partner/director)
- Controlling Authority obtains NOC from District SP
- Physical standards for guards prescribed
- Supervisors: 1 per 15 guards; if different premises, at least 1 per 6 guards
- Licensee training as prescribed by Controlling Authority
- Renewal: same fee as grant
- Renewal conditions: maintain principal place of business in Karnataka; continue training; police have no objection
- **Karnataka state fee for entire state: ₹50,000** (as per multiple sources including Psaraonline.in)

#### ANDHRA PRADESH
**Source:** http://www.eagle4ss.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/andrapradesh_2005..pdf
- Rules: Andhra Pradesh Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Rules, 2008
- Application: Form V (new license), Form V(a) (renewal)
- Required: Form I + Form III (to be issued by village secretary and attested by Tahsildar) for antecedent verification
- Licensee must successfully undergo 6 days training at Andhra Pradesh Police Academy, Hyderabad
- Renewal fee: 50% of the fee paid for grant of licence
- Must pay minimum wages, comply with PF, GLI, gratuity, labour laws
- Licensee must intimate any criminal charge framed immediately
- Appeal fee: ₹2,500
- Penalties: Contravention of Rule 4 = fine up to ₹10,000 per instance + cancellation; Rules 5/6/7/10/11/12(1) = fine up to ₹5,000 per instance + cancellation

## 5. BUSINESS STRUCTURE DIFFERENCES

**Source:** https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/psara-license-requirements-documents-eligibility-guide.html
**Source:** https://legaldev.in/psara-license

### SOLE PROPRIETORSHIP
- Simplest structure, suitable for small local operations
- Owner personally liable for all business obligations
- Documents: Proprietor's PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, GST registration, Shop & Establishment registration, office proof
- Suitable for: small local operations, individual starting alone
- Limitation: less credibility for corporate contracts

### PARTNERSHIP FIRM
- Two or more persons agree to carry on business together
- Governed by Partnership Deed (profit sharing, capital, roles)
- All partners must meet eligibility requirements and undergo police verification
- Documents: Partnership deed, firm registration certificate, PAN, GST, office proof, KYC of all partners
- Suitable for: 2+ persons wanting joint management

### LIMITED LIABILITY PARTNERSHIP (LLP)
- Provides limited liability protection to partners
- Separate legal identity
- LLP Agreement must include security services/manpower supply/guarding services
- Designated partners: identity proof, address proof, photographs, police verification
- Preferred for: agencies wanting flexibility with legal protection
- Better credibility than proprietorship/partnership

### PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY
- Most preferred structure for corporate clients, industrial units, large institutions, government tenders
- Separate legal identity, limited liability, better credibility, structured governance
- Documents: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA, AOA
- Main object clause must clearly allow private security agency services
- All directors must satisfy eligibility and verification requirements
- Suitable for: large operations, corporate contracts, scalability

### KEY DIFFERENCES FOR PSARA APPLICATION:

| Aspect | Proprietorship | Partnership | LLP | Pvt Ltd |
|--------|---------------|-------------|-----|---------|
| Liability | Unlimited | Unlimited (joint) | Limited | Limited |
| Legal Identity | Not separate | Not separate | Separate | Separate |
| Documents for PSARA | PAN, GST, Shop & Est. | Partnership deed, firm PAN, KYC | Incorp certificate, LLP agreement, PAN | COI, MOA, AOA, PAN, board resolution |
| Credibility | Low | Medium | High | Highest |
| Client Preference | Small clients | Small-medium | Medium-large | Corporate/govt |
| Ownership change impact | License void on conversion | License issues on reconstitution | May need amendment | License void on conversion |
| Police Verification | Proprietor only | All partners | Designated partners | All directors |

**IMPORTANT:** Converting proprietorship to Pvt Ltd company requires completely FRESH PSARA license — old license becomes void.

## 8. POLICE VERIFICATION PROCESS

**Source:** https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/psara-license-india-complete-guide-security-agencies.html
**Source:** https://haryanapolice.gov.in/pdf/How_to_APPLY_for_PSARA_licence.pdf

### Who Needs Police Verification?
- Proprietor (for proprietorship)
- All partners (for partnership firm)
- All directors (for company)
- Majority shareholders
- Key managerial persons
- Principal Officer
- All security guards and supervisors

### Process:
1. Form I / Form II (antecedent verification) submitted with application
2. If company/firm/association → separate Form I/II for each proprietor/partner/director
3. Controlling Authority sends copy to District SP/DCP for verification
4. Local police verify: identity, address, criminal background, character
5. Process uses CCTNS (Crime and Criminal Tracking Networks and Systems)
6. Also uses ICJS (Interoperable Criminal Justice System)
7. Verification report/NOC issued back to Controlling Authority
8. Police verification often takes 15–45 days (most time-consuming stage)

### Required Documents for Police Verification:
- PAN Card
- Aadhaar Card
- Address proof
- Photographs
- Residential history (last few years)
- Affidavit confirming no criminal record

### For Security Guards/Supervisors:
- Form V for character/antecedent verification (in some states)
- Form VI Affidavit
- Must be conducted BEFORE deployment
- Verification reports should be maintained in agency records
- Some states conduct verification through online portals or prescribed forms

### Key Points:
- Residents of other states may need to enclose fee for verification in their home state
- Time varies significantly by state and local police department
- Clean police verification is critical for license approval
- Adverse report → rejection or delay

### When to Renew:
- Apply at least **45 days BEFORE expiry** (some sources: 60-90 days)
- If application is filed after 45-day window → treated as fresh application
- Operating after expiry = illegal (punishable with imprisonment up to 1 year, fine up to ₹25,000)

### Renewal Process Steps:

**Step 1: Check Expiry Date** → Start planning 60–90 days before

**Step 2: Review Compliance Status**
- Training records, police verification, record maintenance, uniform rules, employee records, client records, labour law compliance
- Fix any gaps before filing

**Step 3: Collect Renewal Documents**
- Original/existing PSARA License
- Updated business registration documents (COI, MOA, AOA, LLP Agreement, partnership deed)
- Updated identity/address proof of key persons
- Updated office address proof (rent agreement/NOC/utility bill)
- Police verification documents (updated if required)
- Training records and MOU with training institute
- Employee/guard details (updated list)
- Affidavits and declarations
- Fee payment receipt

**Step 4: Pay Renewal Fee**
- Same as original license fee (₹5,000/₹10,000/₹25,000 depending on area)

**Step 5: File Renewal Application**
- Form I (or Form V depending on state)
- Online or offline per state procedure
- Within prescribed timeline

**Step 6: Police Verification & Scrutiny**
- Authority may conduct fresh police verification
- Review compliance during previous license period
- Respond quickly to any queries

**Step 7: License Renewal**
- Controlling Authority passes order within 30 days of complete application
- Renewed license continues for further 5 years (1 year in some states)
- Validity counted from expiry date of previous license

## 13. MULTI-STATE LICENSE VS SINGLE STATE LICENSE

### Key Rule:
**PSARA License is STRICTLY STATE-SPECIFIC. There is NO "All India" PSARA License.**

**Source:** https://www.legalraasta.com/blog/types-of-psara-security-licenses-in-india/
**Source:** https://www.legalraasta.com/blog/multi-state-security-agency-licensing/

### Single State License:
- Valid only within the state that issued it
- Can operate in 1 district, 2-5 districts, or entire state
- Cannot operate in another state with same license
- Example: Delhi license = Delhi only, NOT Noida (UP) or Gurgaon (Haryana)

### Multi-State Operations:
- Must obtain SEPARATE PSARA License from EACH state
- Each state has different:
  - Application process
  - Fee structure
  - Document requirements
  - Approved training institutes
  - Police verification procedures
  - Validity periods
  - Compliance requirements

### Challenges for Multi-State Agencies:
- More time required for licensing in each state
- Different renewal timelines
- Varying state regulations
- Separate police verifications in each state
- Higher compliance burden
- Need local office in each state

### Example:
Agency licensed in Delhi wants to serve NCR region:
- Need Delhi PSARA for Delhi clients
- Need UP PSARA for Noida/Ghaziabad clients
- Need Haryana PSARA for Gurgaon clients
- Three separate applications, fees, verifications

### Exception:
If the agency operates within a single state's territory (even multiple districts), one license suffices.

### Important Note:
- Some corporate clients have multi-state operations → agency needs licenses for each state where guards are deployed
- Operating outside approved area = legal and compliance risk

## 15. 50+ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

### BASIC FAQs

**Q1: What is PSARA License?**
A: PSARA License is a mandatory legal permission granted by the State Controlling Authority under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005, authorizing an entity to operate a private security agency in India.

**Q2: What does PSARA stand for?**
A: Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005.

**Q3: Is PSARA License mandatory for security agencies?**
A: Yes, absolutely mandatory. Operating without a PSARA License is a criminal offense under Section 4 of the Act, punishable with imprisonment up to 1 year, fine up to ₹25,000, or both.

**Q4: Who issues PSARA License?**
A: The Controlling Authority appointed by the State Government (typically an officer not below Joint Secretary rank in Home Department, or DGP/Commissioner of Police).

**Q5: Can one PSARA License be used across India?**
A: No. PSARA License is strictly state-specific. A separate license is required for each state where you operate.

**Q6: What is the validity of PSARA License?**
A: 5 years in most states. Exception: Uttarakhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh — 1 year validity.

**Q7: Who can apply for PSARA License?**
A: Sole proprietorship, partnership firm, LLP, One Person Company, Private Limited Company, or Public Limited Company — provided the entity is registered in India and meets eligibility conditions.

**Q8: What is the government fee for PSARA License?**
A: ₹5,000 (1 district), ₹10,000 (2-5 districts), ₹25,000 (entire state). Karnataka entire state fee: ₹50,000.

**Q9: How long does it take to get PSARA License?**
A: Typically 45–90 days, depending on state, document readiness, and police verification speed.

**Q10: What documents are needed for PSARA License?**
A: Business registration proof, PAN, GST, office address proof, KYC of directors/partners, police verification documents, affidavits, training MOU, photographs, uniform details.

### ELIGIBILITY FAQs

**Q11: Can a foreign national apply for PSARA License?**
A: No. Only Indian citizens or entities registered in India with majority Indian ownership are eligible. FDI up to 49% allowed via approval route (MHA).

**Q12: What is the minimum age for PSARA License?**
A: 18 years (most sources); some sources mention 21 years. Check state-specific requirements.

**Q13: Can someone with a criminal record get PSARA License?**
A: Generally no. Persons convicted of offences with punishment of 2+ years imprisonment are disqualified under Section 6.

**Q14: Is educational qualification required?**
A: 8th standard pass or above (per some state rules). Not mandatory in all states. Special security training certificate is required.

**Q15: Can an NRI apply for PSARA License?**
A: Only if they are Indian citizens and meet all eligibility criteria.

**Q16: Can a company with foreign directors apply?**
A: Company must be registered in India. Majority shareholders/partners/directors must be Indian citizens. FDI approval required if foreign investment.

### PROCESS FAQs

**Q17: How to apply for PSARA License online?**
A: Visit your state's PSARA portal or Controlling Authority website. Some states have online portals (Delhi, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat); others require offline submission.

**Q18: What are Forms I, II, III in PSARA?**
A: Form I = Application for grant of license; Form II = Antecedent verification; Form III = Affidavit under Section 7(2).

**Q19: Is police verification mandatory?**
A: Yes. For all proprietors/partners/directors/key persons. Also for security guards and supervisors before deployment.

**Q20: Can I start operations while application is pending?**
A: No. You must wait for the license to be granted. Operating without license is illegal.

**Q21: What is the role of a PSARA Consultant?**
A: Eligibility assessment, document preparation, drafting affidavits, MOU facilitation, application filing, police verification coordination, query response, follow-up, post-license compliance guidance.

**Q22: Can I apply for PSARA License myself without a consultant?**
A: Yes, but many applications face delays/rejection due to documentation errors, state-specific nuances, and procedural complexities. Professional assistance is recommended.

### DOCUMENTS FAQs

**Q23: Is GST registration mandatory for PSARA?**
A: Mandatory for almost all applicants. Some states require it.

**Q24: Is EPF/ESIC registration required?**
A: Yes, if you employ staff. Labour law compliance is important for PSARA.

**Q25: Do I need a physical office?**
A: Yes. Virtual office not acceptable. Must be commercial premises. Authority may conduct physical site inspection.

**Q26: Is Shop & Establishment registration needed?**
A: Yes, in most states.

**Q27: What is the MOU with training institute?**
A: A Memorandum of Understanding with a government-recognized security training institute, confirming that your guards will receive prescribed training.

**Q28: Do I need to submit guard photos?**
A: Yes, photos in uniform, along with uniform pattern and logo details.

**Q29: Are ITR documents required?**
A: Yes, typically ITR of last 3 years for all directors/partners/proprietor.

### TRAINING FAQs

**Q30: Is training mandatory for security guards?**
A: Yes. Minimum 100 hours classroom + 60 hours field training (20 working days) for entry level. Ex-servicemen: 40+16 hours (7 working days).

**Q31: Who provides the training?**
A: Government-recognized/PSARA-approved training institutes. Each state has its own approved list.

**Q32: Is training required for the licensee (owner/director)?**
A: Yes. Licensee training of minimum 6 working days covering security scenario, legal provisions, PSA management.

**Q33: Are there relaxations for ex-servicemen?**
A: Yes. Condensed course: 40 hours classroom + 16 hours field training (7 working days) instead of 160 hours.

**Q34: Is NSDC training recognized?**
A: Yes. Training must be in accordance with National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF).

### BUSINESS STRUCTURE FAQs

**Q35: Which business structure is best for PSARA?**
A: LLP or Private Limited Company is preferred for corporate clients, government tenders, and scalability.

**Q36: Can a proprietorship get PSARA License?**
A: Yes, but less preferred for large contracts. Many states prefer/require registered entities like LLP or Pvt Ltd.

**Q37: If I convert proprietorship to Pvt Ltd, does PSARA transfer?**
A: No. Old license becomes void. Must apply for fresh license for new entity.

**Q38: Can I change business structure after getting license?**
A: A completely new PSARA application is required as the legal entity changes.

### RENEWAL FAQs

**Q39: When should I renew PSARA License?**
A: At least 45 days before expiry (some states: 90 days). Start planning 60-90 days before.

**Q40: What happens if I miss the renewal deadline?**
A: Must apply for FRESH license. Operating after expiry is illegal.

**Q41: What is the renewal fee?**
A: Same as original license fee. Andhra Pradesh: 50% of grant fee.

**Q42: Can a cancelled license be reinstated?**
A: Generally no. Must apply fresh and meet all eligibility requirements again.

### MULTI-STATE FAQs

**Q43: Can I operate in multiple states with one license?**
A: No. Separate license required for each state.

**Q44: Can I transfer license from one state to another?**
A: No. Must apply fresh in the new state.

### GENERAL FAQs

**Q45: What is the penalty for operating without PSARA?**
A: Imprisonment up to 1 year, fine up to ₹25,000, or both.

**Q46: Can PSARA License be suspended or cancelled?**
A: Yes. Grounds include: breach of trust, misappropriation, employing unverified guards, non-compliance with training requirements, violation of license conditions.

**Q47: Is FDI allowed in private security agencies?**
A: Yes, up to 49% under approval route. Administered by MHA.

**Q48: Are there uniform requirements for guards?**
A: Yes. Uniform must be distinctly different from defence, paramilitary, or police uniforms. Cap, badges, and logo must be as per prescribed format.

**Q49: Do I need a supervisor for guards?**
A: Yes. Minimum 1 supervisor per 15 guards. In different premises: at least 1 per 6 guards.

**Q50: Can a security agency provide armed guards?**
A: Yes, if agency holds appropriate arms license and complies with Arms Act requirements.

**Q51: What records must a licensed agency maintain?**
A: Register of: persons managing the agency, guards/supervisors (names, addresses, photos, salaries), clients served.

**Q52: Is the PSARA license transferable?**
A: No. License is granted to specific entity/person and cannot be transferred.

### CITY-SPECIFIC FAQs

**Q53: How to get PSARA License in Jaipur?**
A: Apply to Rajasthan Controlling Authority (Home Department). Process: 45-60 days. Local office proof in Rajasthan required. Training MOU mandatory.

**Q54: How to get PSARA License in Delhi?**
A: Apply through Delhi Police PSARA Portal (online). Fastest processing: 30-45 days. Digital signatures must match exactly.

**Q55: How to get PSARA License in Gurgaon?**
A: Apply to Haryana Controlling Authority (Addl. DGP Law & Order, Panchkula). Office must be at commercial location in Haryana. Process: 30-45 days.
