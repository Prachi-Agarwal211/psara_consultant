## 7. PSARA License Market Context

- Governing law: Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005
- Issued by: State Controlling Authority (state-specific, not central)
- Validity: 5 years
- Eligibility: Indian citizens only, 18+ years, 8th pass minimum education, clean antecedents
- FDI allowed up to 49% under approval route
- Requirements: Company registration, PAN, GST, PF/ESIC, MOU with training institute, police verification
- Process: Documentation → MOU with training institute → Application filing → Antecedent verification → License issuance
- Timeline: 30–60 working days (varies by state)
