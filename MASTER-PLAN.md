### 4.5 PSARA License Main Guide

**Section 1 — What is PSARA License?**
The PSARA License is a mandatory legal permission granted by the State Controlling Authority under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005, authorizing an entity to operate a private security agency in India. It is required for any person, firm, LLP, or company providing security guards, supervisors, bodyguards, bouncers, or armed guards.

**Section 2 — Who Needs PSARA License?**
- Security guard agencies and manpower suppliers
- Bouncer and bodyguard service providers
- Event security agencies
- Corporate security providers
- Agencies serving offices, factories, malls, hotels, hospitals, housing societies

**Section 3 — 11-Step Process**
1. Decide Business Structure (Proprietorship/Partnership/LLP/Pvt Ltd)
2. Include Security Services in Business Objects (MOA must include security services clause)
3. Arrange Office Premises (Commercial, with proper documentation)
4. Prepare Business Documents (PAN, GST, incorporation, bank account)
5. Police Verification of directors/partners/proprietor
6. Training MOU with PSARA-approved institute
7. Appoint supervisors (1 per 15 guards)
8. Prepare affidavits (Section 7(2), eligibility, non-criminal)
9. File application with Controlling Authority + fee
10. Scrutiny by Authority (queries + responses)
11. License granted (valid for 5 years / 1 year for some states)

**Section 4 — Eligibility Criteria**
3 cards: For Agency | For Directors/Key Persons | For Security Guards

**Section 5 — Government Fee Structure**
Table: One District ₹5,000 | 2-5 Districts ₹10,000 | Entire State ₹25,000 | Karnataka ₹50,000

**Section 6 — Timeline**
Overall 45-90 days | Document prep 7-15 days | Training MOU 3-10 days | Filing 1-3 days | Police verification 15-45 days | Scrutiny 15-30 days

**Section 7 — Validity**
Most states: 5 years. Uttarakhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh: 1 year.

### 4.10 Renewal Process Page

**When to Renew:** At least 45 days before expiry. Some states require 60-90 days. Operating after expiry = illegal.

**7-Step Renewal Process:**
1. Check Expiry Date — Start 60-90 days before
2. Review Compliance Status — Training records, police verification, employee records, labour compliance
3. Collect Renewal Documents — Original license, updated business docs, KYC, address proof, police verification, training records, affidavits
4. Pay Renewal Fee — Same as original. AP: 50% of grant fee
5. File Renewal Application — Form I or Form V
6. Police Verification & Scrutiny — Authority may conduct fresh verification
7. License Renewed — Order within 30 days. 5 years validity (1 year for some states)

**Documents Required for Renewal:**
16 items listed (original license, PAN, ID proof, address proof, affidavits, logo, Shop & Est, EPF, ESIC, training affidavit, arms license, employee records, ITR, COI, MOA, guard details, updated MOU, bank statements)

**Consequences of Non-Renewal:**
- Agency treated as un-licensed after expiry
- Must apply for FRESH license
- Operating without license = imprisonment up to 1 year
- Loss of contracts and reputation

### 4.12 Multi-State vs Single State Page

**Key Rule:** There is NO "All India" PSARA License. PSARA is state-specific. Separate license required for each state.

**Single State License:**
- Valid only within issuing state
- Can operate in 1 district, 2-5 districts, or entire state

**Multi-State Operations:**
- MUST obtain separate license from EACH state
- Each state has different process, fee, documents, training institutes, police verification, validity

**Example: NCR Region**
A Delhi-licensed agency wanting to serve NCR needs:
- Delhi PSARA → Delhi clients
- UP PSARA → Noida/Ghaziabad
- Haryana PSARA → Gurgaon
Three separate applications, fees, verifications.

**Challenges for Multi-State:**
- More time for each state
- Different renewal timelines
- Varying regulations
- Separate police verifications
- Local office in each state

**Who Needs Verification:**
Proprietor, all partners, all directors, majority shareholders, key managers, principal officer, all guards and supervisors

**4-Step Process:**
1. Submit Forms (Form I/Form II)
2. Authority sends to local police
3. Local police investigation (CCTNS, ICJS databases)
4. Verification report sent back

**Timeline:** 15-45 days (most time-consuming stage)

**For Guards/Supervisors:**

- Must be BEFORE deployment

**Tips for Smooth Verification:**
- Matching names/addresses on ALL documents
- Accurate residential history
- Prompt response to police inquiries
- Good character references

## 5. FAQs

### 55+ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What is PSARA License?**
A: PSARA License is a mandatory legal permission granted by the State Controlling Authority under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005, authorizing an entity to operate a private security agency in India.

**Q2: What does PSARA stand for?**
A: Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005.

**Q3: Is PSARA License mandatory for security agencies?**
A: Yes, absolutely mandatory. Operating without a PSARA License is a criminal offense under Section 4 of the Act, punishable with imprisonment up to 1 year, fine up to ₹25,000, or both.

**Q4: Who issues PSARA License?**
A: The Controlling Authority appointed by the State Government — typically an officer not below Joint Secretary rank in Home Department, or DGP/Commissioner of Police.

**Q5: Can one PSARA License be used across India?**
A: No. PSARA License is strictly state-specific. A separate license is required for each state where you operate.

**Q6: What is the validity of PSARA License?**
A: 5 years in most states. Exception: Uttarakhand, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh — 1 year validity.

**Q7: Who can apply for PSARA License?**
A: Sole proprietorship, partnership firm, LLP, One Person Company, Private Limited Company, or Public Limited Company — provided the entity is registered in India and meets eligibility conditions.

**Q8: What is the government fee for PSARA License?**
A: ₹5,000 (1 district), ₹10,000 (2-5 districts), ₹25,000 (entire state). Karnataka entire state fee: ₹50,000.

**Q9: How long does it take to get PSARA License?**
A: Typically 45–90 days, depending on state, document readiness, and police verification speed.

**Q10: What documents are needed for PSARA License?**
A: Business registration proof, PAN, GST, office address proof, KYC of directors/partners, police verification documents, affidavits, training MOU, photographs, uniform details.

**Q11: Can a foreign national apply for PSARA License?**
A: No. Only Indian citizens or entities registered in India with majority Indian ownership are eligible. FDI up to 49% allowed via approval route.

**Q12: What is the minimum age for PSARA License?**
A: 18 years (most sources). Check state-specific requirements as some states mention 21 years.

**Q13: Can someone with a criminal record get PSARA License?**
A: Generally no. Persons convicted of offences with punishment of 2+ years imprisonment are disqualified under Section 6 of the Act.

**Q14: Is educational qualification required?**
A: 8th standard pass or above in some states. A special security training certificate is required for key personnel.

**Q15: Can an NRI apply for PSARA License?**
A: Only if they are Indian citizens and meet all eligibility criteria including police verification.

**Q16: How to apply for PSARA License online?**
A: Visit your state's PSARA portal or Controlling Authority website. Some states (Delhi, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat) have online portals; others require offline submission.

**Q17: What are Forms I, II, III in PSARA?**
A: Form I = Application for grant of license; Form II = Antecedent verification; Form III = Affidavit under Section 7(2) of the PSARA Act.

**Q18: Is police verification mandatory?**
A: Yes. For all proprietors, partners, directors, and key persons. Also for security guards and supervisors before deployment.

**Q19: Can I start operations while application is pending?**
A: No. You must wait for the license to be granted. Operating without license is illegal under Section 4 of the Act.

**Q20: What is the role of a PSARA Consultant?**
A: Eligibility assessment, document preparation, drafting affidavits, MOU facilitation, application filing, police verification coordination, query response, follow-up, and post-license compliance guidance.

**Q21: Can I apply for PSARA License myself without a consultant?**
A: Yes, but many applications face delays or rejection due to documentation errors, state-specific nuances, and procedural complexities.

**Q22: Is GST registration mandatory for PSARA?**
A: Yes, mandatory for almost all applicants. Most states require GST registration as a prerequisite.

**Q23: Is EPF/ESIC registration required?**
A: Yes, if you employ staff. Labour law compliance is important for PSARA license approval.

**Q24: Do I need a physical office?**
A: Yes. Virtual office is not acceptable. Must be commercial premises. Authority may conduct physical site inspection. PSARA Consultant India provides ready-to-use commercial office addresses in Jaipur, Delhi, and Gurgaon for clients who don't have their own premises.

**Q25: Is Shop & Establishment registration needed?**
A: Yes, in most states. It is a mandatory registration for security agencies.

**Q26: What is the MOU with training institute?**
A: A Memorandum of Understanding with a government-recognized security training institute, confirming that your guards will receive prescribed training as per PSARA rules.

**Q27: Do I need to submit guard photos?**
A: Yes, photos in uniform, along with uniform pattern and logo details as per the prescribed format.

**Q28: Are ITR documents required?**
A: Yes, typically ITR of last 3 years for all directors, partners, or proprietor as financial capacity proof.

**Q29: Is training mandatory for security guards?**
A: Yes. Minimum 100 hours classroom + 60 hours field training (20 working days) for entry level. Ex-servicemen: 40+16 hours (7 working days).

**Q30: Who provides the training?**
A: Government-recognized or PSARA-approved training institutes. Each state maintains its own approved list of institutes.

**Q31: Is training required for the licensee?**
A: Yes. Licensee training of minimum 6 working days covering security scenario, legal provisions, and PSA management.

**Q32: Are there relaxations for ex-servicemen?**
A: Yes. Condensed course: 40 hours classroom + 16 hours field training (7 working days) instead of 160 hours.

**Q33: Is NSDC training recognized?**
A: Yes. Training must be in accordance with National Skill Qualification Framework (NSQF) as per NSDC guidelines.

**Q34: Which business structure is best for PSARA?**
A: LLP or Private Limited Company is preferred for corporate clients, government tenders, and scalability.

**Q35: Can a proprietorship get PSARA License?**
A: Yes, but less preferred for large contracts. Many states prefer registered entities like LLP or Pvt Ltd.

**Q36: If I convert proprietorship to Pvt Ltd, does PSARA transfer?**
A: No. Old license becomes void. Must apply for a fresh license for the new entity.

**Q37: When should I renew PSARA License?**
A: At least 45 days before expiry. Some states require 60-90 days. Start planning 60-90 days before expiry.

**Q38: What happens if I miss the renewal deadline?**
A: Must apply for a FRESH license. Operating after expiry is illegal and punishable under the Act.

**Q39: What is the renewal fee?**
A: Same as original license fee. Andhra Pradesh: 50% of grant fee.

**Q40: Can a cancelled license be reinstated?**
A: Generally no. Must apply fresh and meet all eligibility requirements again.

**Q41: Can I operate in multiple states with one license?**
A: No. Separate license required for each state. There is no all-India PSARA license.

**Q42: Can I transfer license from one state to another?**
A: No. Must apply fresh in the new state with local office and documentation.

**Q43: What is the penalty for operating without PSARA?**
A: Imprisonment up to 1 year, fine up to ₹25,000, or both under Section 20 of the Act.

**Q44: Can PSARA License be suspended or cancelled?**
A: Yes. Grounds include breach of trust, misappropriation, employing unverified guards, non-compliance with training, violation of license conditions.

**Q45: Is FDI allowed in private security agencies?**
A: Yes, up to 49% under government approval route, administered by MHA.

**Q46: Are there uniform requirements for guards?**
A: Yes. Uniform must be distinctly different from defence, paramilitary, or police uniforms. Cap, badges, and logo as per prescribed format.

**Q47: Do I need a supervisor for guards?**
A: Yes. Minimum 1 supervisor per 15 guards. In different premises: at least 1 per 6 guards.

**Q48: Can a security agency provide armed guards?**
A: Yes, if the agency holds an appropriate arms license and complies with the Arms Act requirements.

**Q49: What records must a licensed agency maintain?**
A: Register of persons managing the agency, guards/supervisors details (names, addresses, photos, salaries), and client details.

**Q50: Is the PSARA license transferable?**
A: No. License is granted to a specific entity or person and cannot be transferred.

**Q51: How to get PSARA License in Jaipur?**
A: Apply to Rajasthan Controlling Authority (Home Department). Process: 45-60 days. Local office proof in Rajasthan required.

**Q52: How to get PSARA License in Delhi?**
A: Apply through Delhi Police PSARA Portal (online). Fastest processing: 30-45 days. Digital signatures must match exactly.

**Q53: How to get PSARA License in Gurgaon?**
A: Apply to Haryana Controlling Authority (Addl. DGP Law & Order, Panchkula). Office must be at commercial location in Haryana. Process: 30-45 days.

**Q54: What is the cost of PSARA License consultant?**
A: Consultant fees range from ₹14,999 to ₹50,000 depending on services included — documentation, MOU, police verification, liaison.

**Q55: How many guards can I deploy with PSARA License?**
A: There is no upper limit. You can deploy as many guards as your business requires, subject to supervisor ratio compliance.

**Q56: What is the difference between PSARA License and security guard license?**
A: PSARA License is for the agency itself. Individual guards need training certificates and police verification, not a separate license.

**Q57: Can a company with foreign directors get PSARA License?**
A: Only if majority shareholders/partners/directors are Indian citizens. FDI approval needed for >49% foreign investment (max 49% allowed).

**Q58: Is there a central PSARA license for all states?**
A: No. PSARA is state-specific. Each state has its own Controlling Authority issuing separate licenses.

**Q59: What documents are needed to show financial capacity?**
A: ITR of last 3 years, bank statements, net worth certificate, CA certificate of financial standing.

**Q60: Can I apply for PSARA renewal online?**
A: Depends on the state. Delhi, Karnataka, Gujarat, Maharashtra have online renewal portals. Others require offline submission.

### A3. Complete PSARA License Process (from Multiple Sources)

**Step 0: Determine Licensing Category**
- One District: Lowest fees, small bond
- Up to Five Districts: Moderate fees, regional
- State-wide: Highest fees, ₹5 lakh bond, multi-city contracts

**Step 1: Business Registration**
- Entity registration with MCA (LLP/Pvt Ltd recommended)
- PAN, TAN, GST, Shop & Est, EPF, ESIC, MSME
- ⚠️ **Critical**: Object clause must include "Security Services"

**Step 2: Office Setup**
- Commercial premises (NOT residential/virtual)
- Rent agreement, electricity bill, NOC from owner, photos
- Address must match EXACTLY across ALL documents

**Step 3: Police Character Verification (START FIRST!)**
- Takes 45-60 days — this is the #1 bottleneck
- Submit to local police station
- CCTNS + ICJS database check
- Required for proprietors, partners, directors, key persons

**Step 4: Training MOU**
- Sign MOU with PSARA-approved training institute
- MHA maintains approved list at psara.gov.in
- MOU covers guard training, supervisor training, licensee training
- Without valid MOU → application may be rejected

**Step 5: Document Preparation**
- All KYC docs, affidavits (Section 7(2)), Form I, II, III
- Uniform photos, logo, ID card samples
- Guard details, supervisor structure, client pipeline

**Step 6: Application Submission**
- Form I + Form II + Form III + all documents + fee
- Online in 14 states; offline in others
- Fee: ₹5K/₹10K/₹25K (₹50K for Karnataka state-wide)

**Step 7: Site Inspection**
- Authority inspects office premises
- Verifies infrastructure, staff records, training setups

**Step 8: Scrutiny & Interview**
- Controlling Authority reviews
- May call for personal interview
- Order must be passed within 60 days of complete application

**Step 9: License Grant**
- Valid for 5 years (most states)
- Must display at place of business
- Agency must commence operations within 6 months

**Total Timeline:** 45-90 days (up to 120 days in some states)

### A4. Top Application Rejection Reasons (Ranked by Frequency)

1. **Address Discrepancies (40%+ rejection cases)** — GST certificate, business registration, lease agreement having even slightly different addresses. This is the #1 reason for PSARA application delays and rejections.
2. **Company Name Errors** — Name doesn't include "Security Agency" or "Security Services". Using prohibited words (National, Indian, Detective, Intelligence, Investigation, Labour Supplier).
3. **Missing or Invalid MOU** — MOU from a training center removed from MHA's approved list. MOU not signed properly or missing essential clauses.
4. **Police Verification Issues** — Criminal background, pending court cases, links with banned organizations. Directors changed mid-application without updating verification.
5. **Incomplete Documentation** — Missing affidavits, expired address proof (electricity bill older than 2 months), unclear photographs, object clause missing security services.
6. **Inadequate Financial Proof** — ITR not filed for 3 years, insufficient bank statements, no proof of financial standing.
7. **Poor Office Setup** — Residential address instead of commercial, no proper infrastructure, virtual office not acceptable.
8. **Wrong Area of Operation** — Selected 1 district but client is in another. Wrong district names. Type cannot be modified after submission in some states.
9. **Training Non-Compliance** — Guards not trained, training from unapproved institute, missing training certificates.
10. **Operating Without License** — Starting deployment before license approval ("testing waters"). This can lead to criminal prosecution.
