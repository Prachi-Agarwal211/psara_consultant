## 2. Color System (Cinematic Design)

Based on the LeftClick/Splintr design prompt aesthetic presets:

### 2.1 Preset Option A: Midnight Luxe (Recommended for PSARA)
- Dark, authoritative, premium feel — fits security/legal consulting
- Deep navy backgrounds with gold accents
- Glass morphism cards with subtle glow effects

### 2.2 Preset Option B: Brutalist Signal
- Bold, high-contrast, raw precision
- Black + white + one accent color
- Heavy typography, minimal imagery

### 2.3 Preset Option C: Warm Commerce
- Trustworthy, approachable, professional
- Warm neutrals with blue accents
- Softer, more corporate feel

**Recommendation:** Midnight Luxe for PSARA — security/legal authority demands premium, trustworthy dark design with gold accents.
